PROVENANCE OF THE STANDARD EM-ONLY JES MAP (jes_full_v1, free 2, with its error)
=============================================================================
Made the standard by Sean on 2026-11-25. It replaces jesmap_pixring_v1, whose
tracked copy and provenance are in the git history of this folder (commits
b784c34, 5923272, 6526e1b). The new map comes from a sample with vertex
emulation, which the old one did not have.

Map file: JetEnergyScale_lookup_em_only.json (this folder). It is
  /star/data01/pwg/seanp/jes_points/jesmap_full_v1/JetEnergyScale_lookup_em_only.json
  byte for byte, with the error arrays (jes_error_note, jes_error_E,
  jes_error_stat, jes_error_sys, jes_error_total) inserted before its final
  brace by jes_map_compare.py error --write-map
  (copy at jes_points/jes_generator_sys_v1/JetEnergyScale_lookup_em_only_witherror.json).
JSON metadata: model logcubic, jet_type em, free_params 2, sample
  jes_full_v1, k 0.947, tune "pythia6 PyTune 370, FMS tune", commit ae7229e.

3. ERROR ARRAYS (jes_error_*)
----------------------------
These give the relative error of F(E) at the energies 3-250 GeV in
  jes_error_E. AnHistograms --jes-error and ApplyCorrections --jes-shift read
  jes_error_E and jes_error_total.
stat: the map's own noise. 14 random per-ptHat-bin subset maps of
  jes_full_v1/flat (k = 2/4/8, seed 1, same vertex emulation; clusters
  1283198-1283693) were analysed with jes_map_compare.py noise, giving
  jes_points/jes_subsets_full_v1/noise_sigma.csv. The error is 0.51% averaged
  over 3-100 GeV.
sys: the generator systematic, from PYTHIA 8 Detroit (Tune:pp 33, default
  PYTHIA 8 PDF).
  Sample: jes_points/jes_p8detroit_v1, 1000 jobs x 2000 events, clusters
    1453030-1453039. 28 jobs were truncated, leaving 972 good jobs (1.944M
    events).
  Map: jes_points/jesmap_p8detroit_v1, cluster 1463185, with the standard
    settings plus -F 2 -X "0:5:31.499". That option uses PYTHIA 6's sigma for
    the ptHat 0-5 bin in place of PYTHIA 8's 161 mb (Sean 2026-11-24).
  The systematic is the noise-subtracted RMS over the grid of the smoothed
    (5-bin Gaussian) difference (jes_map_compare.py error).
  Values: 5.9% at 3 GeV, about 3.8% at 8-20 GeV, 1.1% at 70 GeV, 2.6% at 100 GeV.
  Above Emax (about 107 GeV) the values are extrapolation.
total = stat (+) sys.
Command (2026-11-25):
  jes_map_compare.py error --base jesmap_full_v1/JetEnergyScale_lookup_em_only.json
    --sigma jes_subsets_full_v1/noise_sigma.csv --nbase 9830000
    --alt P8Detroit=jesmap_p8detroit_v1/JetEnergyScale_lookup_em_only.json:1944000
    --alt-dir P8Detroit=jesmap_p8detroit_v1 --out jes_generator_sys_v1/jes_error_em_only
    --write-map jes_generator_sys_v1/JetEnergyScale_lookup_em_only_witherror.json
Plots: jes_generator_sys_v1/jes_error_em_only_{sys,total}.png and
  jes_generator_sys_vs_E.png.
Not in the error arrays: the point-threshold maps
  (jes_points/jesmap_pointthr_v1, same-events difference on this map). They
  are run as separate analysis variations.
