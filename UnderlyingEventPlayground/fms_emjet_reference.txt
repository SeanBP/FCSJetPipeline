2. RECO INPUT
-------------
Input objects             FMS points (photon candidates), not tower hits.
                          Hits are an option (useFmsHit) that is commented out.
                          StRoot/StJetMaker/mudst/StjFMSMuDst.cxx
Detectors                 FMS only (detId 8-11); useEmJetMode = true drops all
                          TPC tracks from the jet input; BEMC off
Point energy cut          1.0 < E < 100 GeV       EjAna.cxx (kFmsEngMin/Max)
                          - 0.5 GeV used for a cross-check against another analysis
                          - simulation config uses 1.0 < E < 200 GeV
                          - their comment: the 1 GeV cut rejects low-energy
                            photons / hadronic contributions and matches data
                            much better; 3 GeV was the earlier calibration-era value
                          - StjFMSMuDst also has a hard floor of 0.1 GeV
Point four-vector         massless, direction = (point position) - (event vertex)
                          StRoot/StJetMaker/towers/StjTowerEnergyToTLorentzVector.cxx
Hadronic correction       none in effect (no tracks in EM-jet mode)
Gain correction           Run 15: fmsGainCorr-BNL-C from DB, plus a manual
                          hot/bad channel mask per run (TStFmsHotChDB)
                          Run 17: local text file
                          Simulation: fmsGainCorr-BNL-C


3. VERTEX

                          without slewing, else 0

                          StRoot/StJetMaker/StEmVertexMaker.cxx

5. UNDERLYING-EVENT SUBTRACTION
-------------------------------
Method                    off-axis cones (STAR method, G. Webb)
                          StRoot/StJetMaker/StJetMaker2012.cxx (addJetUe)
Cones                     2 per jet, at the jet's eta, phi = phi_jet +/- pi/2
Cone radius               0.7 (same as jet R)     StOffAxisConesPars(kRMax)
Cone contents             the same particle list given to the jet finder
                          (after the input cuts of section 2, or the truth
                          photons of section 4). A particle is assigned to
                          cone 0 if within its radius, otherwise tested
                          against cone 1.
Density                   rho = mean over the 2 cones of
                                (sum of constituent pT in cone) / (pi * R^2)
Correction                dpT = rho * (jet area);  pT_corr = pT - dpT
                          StRoot/BrJetMaker/TStNanoJetTreeMaker.cxx (SetUedPt)
                          emJetAnalysis/src/EjCreateBinnedHist.cxx
What is corrected         jet pT only. Jet energy is not UE-subtracted.
Levels                    applied to detector AND particle jets; the pT
                          response matrix is filled with (pT - dpT) on both axes
                          starSim/src/FmsSimMakeResponseMatrix.cxx
Order                     UE subtraction -> pT/E correction (section 7) ->
                          pT > 2 GeV cut
EEMC                      not applied for the EEMC version of the analysis
QA range                  dpT histogrammed over 0-2 GeV

10. HOW FCSJetPipeline COMPARES (EM-only point jets, A_N chain; updated 2026-11-03)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Sections 1-9 are the FMS analysis as read from its code. Below, the FCS
pipeline as it stands (RecoJets / JetMatcher -E 1 -F 1, UnderlyingEvent,
ApplyCorrections_em_only, asymmetry/ AnHistograms + AnAsymmetry).

Same as FMS:
  - Jets: anti-kT, R = 0.7, E-scheme, active areas (ghost area 0.04, |y| < 5).
  - Reco input: points, E > 1 GeV (FMS code; the FMS analysis note says
    E > 2 GeV and E_T > 0.2 GeV) with an upper limit at one beam's energy
    (FMS 100 GeV at 200 GeV; here sqrt(s)/2 = 254 GeV).
  - Jet pT at finding: 2 GeV reco (uncorrected), 1 GeV truth (JES sample: none).
  - Truth jets from photons only.
  - Order: UE subtraction, then energy correction, then the 2 GeV pT cut on the
    corrected jet, and E <= sqrt(s)/2, 0 < x_F <= 1 on the corrected jet.
  - Trigger rule: a jet passes if its pT exceeds 1.15 x the nominal threshold
    of a trigger that recorded the event (FMS "extended" version: raw pT, as
    here; FMS base version: corrected pT).
  - LED events vetoed; both beams polarized (spin4 = 5, 6, 9, 10), which also
    removes abort-gap and single-beam bunches (run 23007048: 8 of 51411 events
    have spin4 1, 4 or 8, none other).
  - Photons per jet: the jet's point constituents (points > 1 GeV), as FMS
    (since 2026-11-05; AnHistograms reads reco_nconst, and RecoJets/JetMatcher
    now fill reco_nphotons with it for point jets; before, reco_nphotons was
    the points within R of the axis, ~2.5% of jets different). Never 0 for a
    found jet; classes 1, 2, 3, 4, >= 5 kept separately, to be grouped as FMS
    (1-2, 3, ...).
  - A_N reported for blue at x_F > 0 and yellow at x_F < 0, |x_F| from the
    corrected energy, polarization weighted per run by the jets.
  - PYTHIA 6.4.28, PyTune 370, PARP(90) = 0.213, PARP(91) = 1, CTEQ6L1, MSEL 1
    (FMS tune) for the simulation behind the JES.

Different on purpose:
  - UE: energy subtracted (eta-weighted side wedges, 2R to 150 deg, the jet's
    own event, area-matched), FMS: pT subtracted with two cones at +-90 deg.
    The FCS has no full azimuth.
  - Energy correction: (x, y, E) log-cubic map from numerical inversion
    (jesmap_pixring_v1), FMS: 1D polynomials in E and pT.
  - Acceptance: jet axis in the ECal rectangle with a 20 cm buffer (eta about
    2.3-3.6), FMS: 2.8 < eta < 3.8.
  - Triggers: fcsEM0/1/2 (and _tpc copies; nominal 2.0 / 3.5 / 5.0 GeV E_T),
    FMS: JP0-2 and board sums. FCS jet patches add HCal energy, so they are
    not used for EM jets.
  - A_N: left-right cross ratio with <|cos phi|> correction, the other beam's
    spin held fixed; FMS: cross ratio in 16 phi bins fitted with
    p0 cos(phi) + p1, the other beam summed.
  - Binning: x_F bins of 0.05 (FMS note also in x_F; FMS code bins in E and pT).
  - Matching (JES only): one-to-one, dR < R/2; FMS: nearest within 0.2.
  - Simulation: ptHat bins with cross-section weights and no generator
    filter, sqrt(s) 508.4 GeV, ECal scale k = 0.947; FMS: flat ptHat 2-80 GeV
    with a generator and an E_T filter.
  - Truth photons: GEANT photons made within 0.01 cm of the primary vertex
    (pi0/eta decayed by GEANT); FMS: PYTHIA status-1 photons (pi0/eta decayed
    by PYTHIA). Long-lived decays (K0S, Lambda) excluded in both.
  - Run list: catalog runs 23005044-23108024 minus MakeRunList.pl ranges and
    RunLog flags (to 23027047), minus D. Kapukchyan's pi0 bad-run list
    (AnAsymmetry -r); FMS: its own run list and fill 18764.

Not the same, to check:
  - Vertex: no vertex used here (the FCS data has BBC vertices in about 37% of
    events, no TPC/VPD in st_fwd); directions from the origin in data and
    simulation. FMS: |vz| < 80 cm and directions from the vertex. The
    simulation's vertex (Gaussian at 0, sigma 30 cm) does not match the data:
    BBC vertices of run 23007048 have mean -15.8 cm, RMS 17.2 cm (BBC
    resolution included). FMS drew simulated vertices from data.
  - Hot / bad channels: FMS masked them per run (TStFmsHotChDB); here only the
    DB's gains and the point energy upper limit act on them; no explicit mask.

Not applied in either (systematics in the FMS note):
  - Ring-of-fire cut (FMS: stored, cut commented out; varied as a systematic),
    pile-up, polarization scale.
  - Unfolding: FMS SVD (RooUnfold) on E, pT and photon multiplicity; here not
    yet.
