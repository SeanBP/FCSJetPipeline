5. UNDERLYING-EVENT SUBTRACTION
-------------------------------
Method                    off-axis cones (STAR method, G. Webb)
                          StRoot/StJetMaker/StJetMaker2012.cxx (addJetUe)
Cones                     2 per jet, at the jet's eta, phi = phi_jet +/- pi/2
Cone radius               0.7 (same as jet R)     StOffAxisConesPars(kRMax)
Cone contents             the same particle list given to the jet finder
                          (after the input cuts of section 2, or the truth
                          photons of section 4). A particle is assigned to
                          cone 0 if within its radius, otherwise tested
                          against cone 1.
Density                   rho = mean over the 2 cones of
                                (sum of constituent pT in cone) / (pi * R^2)
Correction                dpT = rho * (jet area);  pT_corr = pT - dpT
                          StRoot/BrJetMaker/TStNanoJetTreeMaker.cxx (SetUedPt)
                          emJetAnalysis/src/EjCreateBinnedHist.cxx
What is corrected         jet pT only. Jet energy is not UE-subtracted.
Levels                    applied to detector AND particle jets; the pT
                          response matrix is filled with (pT - dpT) on both axes
                          starSim/src/FmsSimMakeResponseMatrix.cxx
Order                     UE subtraction -> pT/E correction (section 7) ->
                          pT > 2 GeV cut
EEMC                      not applied for the EEMC version of the analysis
QA range                  dpT histogrammed over 0-2 GeV

10. HOW FCSJetPipeline COMPARES (EM-only, point-based mode: -E 1 -F 1)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Same as FMS:
  - anti-kT, R = 0.7, E-scheme.
  - Reco input: points, not tower hits.
  - Point energy window: E > 1.0 GeV and E <= sqrt(s)/2 (about 254 GeV, the
    counterpart of the FMS 100 GeV limit at sqrt(s) = 200 GeV), through
    pass_point_energy_cut() in simulation and data alike.
  - Truth jets from photons only, no electrons.
  - Truth particles restricted to 1 < eta < 5 (here by the generator macros'
    SetEtaRange, i.e. on what is handed to GEANT).
  - Jet pT minimum at jet finding: 2.0 GeV reco, 1.0 GeV truth.
  - Generator settings, when -M 1 ("FMS tune") is given: PYTHIA 6.4.28,
    PyTune 370, PARP(90) = 0.213, PARP(91) = 1, CTEQ6L1, MSEL 1.

Different from FMS:
  - UE subtraction: energy, not pT. Reco: per jet from proxy events (other
    events of the same run or ptHat bin and EM trigger level, with a jet of
    similar pT near 90 deg from the cone; ../underlying_event/), since the FCS
    does not cover full azimuth. Truth: same-event cones at phi +- 90 deg, as
    FMS. Jet areas are not computed.
  - Jet direction: constituent directions are taken from the origin, as if
    there were no vertex information; event_vertex_z is stored but not used.
  - Truth source: GEANT tracks created within 100 um of the primary vertex
    (STAR's Pythia 6 wrapper leaves pi0/eta stable and GEANT decays them), versus PYTHIA
    status-1 photons with pi0/eta decayed in PYTHIA. Photons from long-lived
    parents (K0S, Lambda, ...) are excluded.
  - (Same as FMS:) truth photons carry no energy cut, for the jets and for
    truth_nphotons, although reco points need 1 GeV. How to cut truth photons
    to match the detector photons is left open, as in FMS.
  - Acceptance: reco jet axis inside the ECAL x-y fiducial rectangle with a
    20 cm buffer (eta 2.34-3.57); truth jet axis within R/2 of that region in
    eta-phi (only jets that could be matched); truth photons in a
    full-azimuth eta band 1.5R beyond its eta range (1.29-4.62).
    FMS: 2.8 < eta < 3.8 on the jet axis.
  - Matching: one-to-one, closest pairs first, dR < R/2 = 0.35 (the CMS
    choice, arXiv:1607.03663), versus nearest within 0.2, not one-to-one.
  - Energy scale: (E, x, y) map with median numerical inversion, versus 1D
    polynomials in E and pT.
  - Unfolding: planned to follow FMS (SVD on the photon-multiplicity
    response); not yet settled (../UnfoldingPlayground/).
  - ptHat: disjoint weighted bins with cross-section weights, versus one flat
    2-80 GeV range. sqrt(s) = 508.4 GeV versus 200 GeV.
  - Generator-level filter: FcsJetFilter (energy on one FCS arm above a
    threshold, default 50 GeV), versus StFmsJetFilter.
  - Simulated vertex: Gaussian (sigma_z = 30 cm), versus a file of data
    vertices.
  - Tower hits (when -F 0): MIP-fraction cut, 0.31 GeV ECAL, no upper cut.

Applied after the pipeline, not in it:
  - Unphysical jets: E <= sqrt(s)/2 and 0 < x_F <= 1 on the final energy,
    after UE subtraction and calibration (pass_physical_jet_cut() in
    JetParameters.h holds the definition).
  - The second 2 GeV jet pT cut, on the corrected pT.
  - Trigger-dependent jet pT thresholds (section 6).
  - Trigger selection: flags are saved in the trees; simulation is produced
    without a trigger requirement.

Not used:
  - Post-detector E_T filter, hot-channel mask in simulation, |vz| cut,
    abort-gap removal, spin-state cut, ring-of-fire cut.

Geometry caution for the off-axis cones: the FMS covers full azimuth, so cones
at phi +/- 90 degrees always land on active detector. The FCS has a gap between
its two halves (ECAL inner edge at |x| ~ 20.5 cm) and ends at y ~ -97 / +86 cm,
so a cone at +/- 90 degrees from a jet near y ~ 0 sits across the gap and
partly off the detector. This is a geometric estimate, not a measured effect.
